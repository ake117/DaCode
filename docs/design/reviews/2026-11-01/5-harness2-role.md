- **Stage:** Comprehension + Role Check

## (a) Is the paper digest self-contained and accurate to its purpose?

1. **Accurate as an origin bookmark, too thin to serve as an algorithmic spec.**
   Taken strictly on its frontmatter metadata (`type: research`, `status: reference`, `created: 2026-10-18` at `docs/design/google-harness2-recursive-agent-harnessing.md:5–16`), the 72-line note accurately records the high-level vocabulary DaCode later adopted: the distinction between a *Harness* and *Harnessing* (`line 32`), the two-level recursion structure (`lines 34–42`), headline 1-step benchmark deltas from `arXiv:2610.14224` (`lines 48–53`), and primary external links (`lines 68–72`). However, it omits *how* Harness² actually constructs zero-ground-truth evaluation signals, contrasts trajectories during Probe, merges candidates during Compose, or prevents regression in the persistent harness. It is self-sufficient as a reading-list summary of why the author became interested in recursive harnessing, not as a technical specification of Layer 2 (`dacode/harness/`).

2. **Dangling internal cross-reference left behind by de-wikilinking.**
   `update_history` (`docs/design/google-harness2-recursive-agent-harnessing.md:8`) states `"2026-10-24: เพิ่ม cross-link ไปยัง note เปรียบเทียบกับ DeepSeek Harness (DSH)"` ("added cross-link to comparison note with DeepSeek Harness (DSH)"), and `## 🔗 Related` (`line 66`) cites `DeepSeek Harness (DSH) — เทียบระเบียบวิธี Harness² กับ runtime dsh (คนละชั้นสถาปัตยกรรม)`. When Obsidian wikilinks were stripped for repository publication, the link target was neither included in `docs/design/` nor removed from `update_history`. A standalone reader encounters a promised cross-link to a missing note and an unexplained parenthetical ("different architectural layer").

3. **No in-file role disclaimer, Thai-only prose in an English codebase, and non-GFM callout syntax.**
   A reader opening `docs/design/google-harness2-recursive-agent-harnessing.md` directly (from search, an IDE tree, or an external link rather than via `docs/design/README.md`) gets no banner clarifying that this is an external paper digest rather than documentation of DaCode—even though `line 60` explicitly names `Pi coding agent` and `Hermes`, both of which appear in `dacode/`. In addition, all prose and section headings (`lines 8–9, 25–66`) are in Thai whereas the rest of the repository's user/developer documentation (`README.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/FAQ.md`, `docs/design/README.md`) is English-only, and `line 25` uses Obsidian's `> [!abstract] สรุปสั้น` callout syntax, which GitHub Flavored Markdown does not support and renders as literal `[!abstract]` text.

## (b) Does `docs/design/README.md` describe the folder contents correctly today?

4. **Incomplete directory inventory (`reviews/` is undocumented).**
   `docs/design/README.md:6–9` presents a two-row table implying `docs/design/` holds only `blueprint-ultimate-autonomous-engineering-rig.md` and `google-harness2-recursive-agent-harnessing.md`. On disk today, `docs/design/` also contains `docs/design/reviews/2026-11-01/` (`1-zoom-out.md`, `2-comprehension.md`, `3-critic.md`, and `5-harness2-role.md`), which `docs/design/README.md` does not mention or scope.

5. **"Verbatim copies … anonymised out" overstates textual fidelity.**
   `docs/design/README.md:3–4` claims the two files are *"Verbatim copies of the two Thai notes this repository grew out of, with personal names, machine names and vault-only wikilinks anonymised out."* Inspection of the files and git history (`6f0e5f7`) shows editorial rewriting beyond identifier anonymization: three of four `Related` entries in `google-harness2-recursive-agent-harnessing.md` and two in `blueprint-ultimate-autonomous-engineering-rig.md` were deleted outright, while `blueprint-ultimate-autonomous-engineering-rig.md:81–82` was rewritten to reference *"โน้ตถัดไปในโฟลเดอร์นี้"* ("the next note in this folder") and drop concrete date/test-count metadata.

6. **Broken topical promise from `docs/FAQ.md` into `docs/design/README.md`.**
   In `docs/FAQ.md:33–39` (*"Is this A/B testing?"*), the FAQ defines DaCode as *"best-of-N selection with a deterministic oracle"* / *"tournament selection"* and points readers to `See [design/README.md](design/README.md)`. Neither `docs/design/README.md` nor either of the two Thai notes it indexes discusses A/B testing, best-of-N selection, tournament selection, or deterministic oracles.

7. **The `What it is` table retrofits shipped implementation details onto the origin notes and overstates Layer 2's fidelity to Harness².**
   - Row 1 (`docs/design/README.md:8`) summarizes the blueprint as specifying *"a static-critic test gate … that DaCode implements"*, whereas `blueprint-ultimate-autonomous-engineering-rig.md:30, 45–46, 65` actually proposed `Obsidian / ChromaDB` for Layer 1 and an adversarial critic *subagent* (`Subagent คอยจับผิดและทดสอบพฤติกรรม`, *"Attack the premise"*) for Layer 4—both of which DaCode replaced with deterministic stdlib components (`dacode/memory/`, `dacode/discipline/critic.py`).
   - Row 2 (`docs/design/README.md:9`) states that DaCode's optimizer and trial loop *"are built on"* Harness²'s Propose–Probe–Compose recursion without noting that DaCode keeps only the stage names and two-level framing while departing sharply from the paper's mechanics (see Findings 8–11).

## (c) Digest claims a reader might mistake for shipped features

8. **Per-variant tool, skill, and memory mutation (`google-harness2-…:26, 32, 38` vs. `dacode/harness/` and `dacode/executor/`).**
   The digest emphasizes that Harness² optimizes the full harness—prompts, tools, memory, and skills—and that `Propose` adds or modifies tools/skills per task (*"ปรับ prompt, เพิ่มเครื่องมือ/skill"*). A reader inspecting `dacode/harness/` can easily assume tool mutation is shipped because `_PROPOSE_SYSTEM` explicitly asks the LLM for `{"label": "<short>", "system_prompt": "<full prompt>", "tools": ["..."]}` (`dacode/harness/optimizer.py:50–51`) and `propose()` parses `tools` into `HarnessVariant.tools` (`dacode/harness/models.py:18`, `dacode/harness/optimizer.py:123`). In reality, **`variant.tools` is a dead field**: `PiExecutor.build_command()` reads `self.tools` (an executor-level tuple defaulting to `()` and never populated by `DaCodeRig._build_executor()`), ignoring `variant.tools` altogether (`dacode/executor/pi.py:51, 59, 91–92`; `dacode/orchestrator.py:99–104`), and `ScriptExecutor.run()` ignores it as well (`dacode/executor/script.py:37–76`). Skills (`skills/`) are never generated or edited, and `memory_snippets` are copied unchanged onto every variant (`dacode/harness/optimizer.py:124`). Only `system_prompt` (plus external `--fleet` / `--escalate-models` model assignment) actually varies across trials.

9. **"Compose" as synthesizing and merging harnesses (`google-harness2-…:40` vs. `dacode/harness/optimizer.py:298–386`).**
   The digest defines `Compose` as synthesizing and merging compared trial results into a single combined harness (*"สังเคราะห์และรวมร่างเป็น harness ที่สมบูรณ์ที่สุดจากผลลัพธ์ที่เปรียบเทียบกัน"*). In `dacode`, `HarnessOptimizer.compose()` never merges prompts, tools, or code changes across variants. It performs winner-take-all `best_id` selection over already-probed variants—via `JevJudge.choice()`, an LLM `best_id` pick, or deterministic `_variant_score` sorting—returns one existing `HarnessVariant` unchanged, and deletes every losing worktree and git branch (`_cleanup_losers()`, `dacode/harness/optimizer.py:438, 543–563`).

10. **Zero ground-truth feedback and contrastive failure-aware revision (`google-harness2-…:26, 37–40` vs. `dacode/discipline/critic.py` and `dacode/harness/optimizer.py:103–111`).**
    Two core algorithmic claims in the digest do not exist in `dacode/`:
    - *Zero ground-truth feedback:* Unlike Harness²'s open-world evaluation without ground truth (*"โดยไม่ต้องอาศัยเฉลย (zero ground-truth feedback)"*, echoed misleadingly in root `README.md:11` as *"No ground-truth labels required"*), DaCode relies entirely on a pre-existing deterministic test oracle (`PstackCritic.review_workspace()` in `dacode/discipline/critic.py`). A variant cannot pass unless a real test command exits `0` with `tests_total >= 1`, `tests_failed == 0`, and no test/infrastructure tampering; `dacode/jeval.py:3` explicitly defines gate-verified passes as *"Ground truth"*, and neither Jev nor the Compose LLM can overrule a gate failure (`dacode/harness/optimizer.py:302–305, 351–355`).
    - *Contrastive multi-round refinement:* When round 1 fails and round 2 calls `propose(task, best, n)`, the prompt constructed in `dacode/harness/optimizer.py:105–110` sends only `task`, `base.system_prompt`, and the static `base.seed_context()`. It passes **zero probe feedback**—no `gate_output`, no `stderr`, no critic `errors` (such as `tests_modified`), no test pass/fail counts, and no comparison against losing variants. Subsequent rounds perform blind prompt re-sampling around `best.system_prompt`, not contrastive failure-driven revision.

11. **Persistent improvement harness updating permanent skills/state and automatic learning from mistakes (`google-harness2-…:41–42, 60` vs. `dacode/orchestrator.py:68–75, 184–246` and `dacode/memory/__init__.py:339–389`).**
    The digest describes updating a *"persistent improvement harness (หน่วยความจำ/ทักษะถาวร)"* (`lines 41–42`, claimed in `ARCHITECTURE.md:74` to be *"exactly the two-level structure described in the Harness² paper"*) and automatically fixing bugs and learning from mistakes (*"แก้บั๊กและเรียนรู้จากความผิดพลาดได้อัตโนมัติ"*, `line 60`). In `dacode/`:
    - If no vault directory is configured or present on disk, `self.memory` is `None` (`dacode/orchestrator.py:68`) and harnessing-level recursion does not run at all.
    - **Failed runs never write lessons to `VaultMemory`**: `DaCodeRig.run_task()` ingests a lesson strictly when `result.passed` is `True` (`dacode/orchestrator.py:210`). Failures are logged only to `runlog.jsonl` and `memory.db` for manual inspection via `dacode state failures` / `dacode retro` and are never automatically fed back into future `run_task()` prompts.
    - Even on passing runs, `VaultMemory.ingest()` writes a passive markdown note under `<vault>/Projects/<repo-name>/Lessons/` without updating `DEFAULT_SYSTEM_PROMPT`, tool configs, or `skills/`. Later runs retrieve at most five 200-character body substrings (`_snippet(..., width=200)` in `dacode/memory/__init__.py:339–351`) appended via `--append-system-prompt`; because `_build_lesson()` places `## System prompt that won` at the very end of the note (`dacode/orchestrator.py:221–240`), a 200-character window matched near the top `## Task` header frequently omits the winning prompt itself. Nor does DaCode auto-apply bug fixes: winning commits remain in `<repo>/../.dacode/worktrees/<trial-id>` awaiting manual merge.

12. **Hermes coding-agent executor, DeepSeek Harness (`dsh`), and paper benchmark/model support (`google-harness2-…:26, 48–53, 60, 66` vs. `dacode/executor/` and `dacode/llm/providers.py`).**
    - `line 60` proposes wrapping coding agents *"เช่น Pi coding agent หรือ Hermes เอง"* in a Propose–Probe–Compose auto-loop. `dacode/executor/` ships only `PiExecutor` and `ScriptExecutor` (`dacode/orchestrator.py:92–104`); there is no Hermes coding-agent executor (Hermes appears only as an optional external vault-search subprocess in `dacode/memory/backends/hermes.py`, a Kanban CLI wrapper in `dacode/kanban.py`, and an external MCP client consumer for `dacode/mcp/server.py`).
    - `line 66` references the DeepSeek Harness (`dsh`) runtime and `lines 48–53` cite WorkBuddy-Bench (`+11.5`), JobBench (`+4.5`), LAB (`+11.8%`), `GLM-5`, and `Gemini 3.5 Flash`. None of `dsh` or those three benchmarks exist in `dacode/` (whose only evaluator is `dacode/jeval.py`), and DaCode's shipped provider presets (`dacode/llm/providers.py:19–66`) default to `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash`, `glm-5.3-flash`, and `gemini-2.5-flash`.

## Verdict

`docs/design/google-harness2-recursive-agent-harnessing.md` succeeds only in its narrow historical role as an archived Thai reading note that inspired DaCode's Layer 2 vocabulary (`Propose–Probe–Compose` and two-level recursion), and it is not fully self-contained even in that role due to a broken `DeepSeek Harness (DSH)` cross-reference, Obsidian-only `[!abstract]` syntax, and the absence of an in-file disclaimer separating external paper claims from DaCode's design. `docs/design/README.md` no longer describes `docs/design/` accurately today: it omits the `reviews/` subtree, overclaims *"verbatim"* preservation despite deleted links and rewritten prose, fails to deliver the tournament-selection explanation promised by `docs/FAQ.md:38–39`, and blurs inspiration with implementation. Most critically, pairing `docs/design/README.md:9` (*"which the optimizer and the trial loop are built on"*) with the digest invites readers to mistake paper mechanisms for shipped `dacode/` behavior—when in fact `dacode` implements single-winner best-of-$N$ prompt selection gated by a mandatory ground-truth test suite, drops `HarnessVariant.tools` on the floor before execution, feeds zero failure output into next-round `propose()`, never auto-learns from failed runs into `VaultMemory`, and ships no `Hermes` executor or harness-merging `Compose`.
